Sajjad Pakdamansavoji

Applied ML researcher — embodied AI, vision-language-action models and 6D pose estimation at Huawei Noah’s Ark Lab.

I am an applied ML researcher and engineer with five years of experience building generative and multimodal systems, focused on turning research in vision-language models and diffusion into systems that hold up outside a benchmark.

At Huawei Noah’s Ark Lab I work on embodied AI pipelines that connect vision to action, bringing diffusion architectures and ViT backbones together with 3D representations inside LLM, VLM and VLA models, to improve physical grounding for spatial reasoning and manipulation.

Before that, at the Vector Institute, I turned LLM and VLM research into agentic systems: multi-step reasoning workflows and RAG pipelines built for retrieval accuracy and safety. I also ran workshops on fine-tuning with LoRA and high-throughput serving with vLLM.

At Trans-Plan I built 3D perception for urban scenes, leading distributed fine-tuning of ViT-based foundation models for open-vocabulary detection, segmentation and tracking. That work raised tracking MOTA by 28% and reached 92% accuracy on trajectory classification, alongside a real-time 3D visualisation tool for human-in-the-loop refinement.

My earlier work at AVIR was a multimodal translation system for Iranian Sign Language, running video-to-text at over 30 FPS.
