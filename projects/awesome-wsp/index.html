What is weight-space programming?

The term comes from the MIT CSAIL report Sparks of Weight-Space Programming (2026-11-02). A general LLM is handed a network's weights, sometimes with code and a budget of forward passes, and edits the numbers itself. In that report a model names what networks were trained on from their weights alone, repairs a stuck control policy by changing a few percent of its weights in one reply, and writes small game-playing networks from zero.

  1. ReadPredict what a network does, or what it was trained on, from its weights.
  2. RepairFix a specific behaviour by editing weights directly.
  3. WriteConstruct a working network from scratch, number by number.
